---
title: Manage environments
description: Start, stop, inspect, and delete Codewire environments
---

Use the organization dashboard to inspect and manage your environments.

## Environment list

Each organization shows its environments with status, repository, preset, recent activity, and billing context.

## Environment details

The detail view contains:

- status and lifecycle progress,
- logs and recent events,
- files and exec access,
- SSH and browser terminal entry points,
- preview ports,
- sessions running inside the environment,
- usage and billing context.

## Lifecycle actions

Owners and admins can start, stop, and delete environments. Members can access environments according to their organization role and explicit access grants.

## Deletion

Deleting an environment removes the sandbox runtime and associated access records. Persistent data tied to that environment is removed as part of teardown.
