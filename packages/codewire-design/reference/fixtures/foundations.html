Foundations

Every value the product uses comes from these tokens in styles.css. Route styles may lay things out but may not introduce a size, colour, radius or spacing of their own.

Type

Six sizes, one per role. Nothing is smaller than 11px, and only the group label uses capitals.

--text-page · 24–30
Sandboxes
--text-title · 17
Credentials
--text-body · 14
Body text and field labels. Labels add weight 500 and the primary colour.
--text-secondary · 13
Descriptions, dialog prose, buttons, navigation and table cells.
--text-small · 12
Field help, errors, metadata and table headers.
--text-micro · 11
Group label
--text-code · 13

Spacing

A field keeps its label, control and help 6px apart; fields sit 20px apart so each label reads with its own control.
