An HTTPS preview forwards requests to an internal Sandbox port. There is no separate external port number. Access controls who may open the preview, not whether a process is listening inside the Sandbox. Organization access also requires access to this Sandbox.

Creator previews still expose an authenticated URL. Remove an entry to disable that HTTPS preview route; removing all entries is not complete network isolation. Public previews may still require sign-in under confidential policy.
