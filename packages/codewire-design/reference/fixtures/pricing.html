Questions

Questions.

Do local VMs cost anything?
No. A local VM runs on your own machine and is never billed. Download the Codewire CLI to run sandboxes as local VMs.
Where do the sandboxes run?
On a private network of bare-metal machines in Amsterdam, Netherlands, and Frankfurt, Germany. Locations in the US are coming soon.
How does billing work?
Your organization keeps a balance. Running sandboxes draw it down per second at the rates above. There are no plans, seats or minimums.
What do I get for free?
Local sandboxes are always free. In the cloud you pay only while your sandboxes run, and adding a card charges nothing. Automatic top-ups add €20 at a time; change the amount or switch them off on the Billing page.
What happens if my balance runs out?
Without a card, or if a top-up fails, new sandboxes and starts are refused at once, and running sandboxes stop after 24 hours. Their storage is kept.
Do credits expire?
No.
Do agents or teammates cost extra?
No. Agents are processes in your sandboxes, and members are free. You pay only for running sandboxes.
How many sandboxes can I run?
Several at once, up to your organization’s limit. Larger limits are available on request, and so are bigger sandboxes and SSO: email hello@codewire.sh.
What if I want to run Codewire myself?
Codewire is open source. You can run it on a Kubernetes cluster. If you’d like our help, email hello@codewire.sh.
